You can't automate signups (ToS + KYC make it pointless). But **people pay a
trusted human to do the setup FOR them, with THEIR OWN identity**. This kit
turns your daily-built expertise into a service gig.

> The golden rule: the buyer's account belongs to the BUYER. You never use
> your own details, and KYC (ID/face checks) is always done by them, on
> camera, live. You sell configuration + education + support, not accounts.

## 4) Buyer onboarding script (5 steps, 15 minutes)

1. Video/audio call or meet — verify THEY own the email/phone used
2. `python signup-helper.py --setup` (on YOUR pc/phone — none of their values
   stay with you; you fill the form live)
3. Pick platforms from `signup-helper.py --list` (sorts reachable first)
4. `python signup-helper.py --fill ysense` → browser opens → walk each field
5. THEY do the captcha + email-verify + later KYC themselves, on their device

Collect testimonial at day 7 (screenshot of first cash notification with
permission) — testimonials triple your close rate.

## 5) Hard boundaries (protects your name)

- ❌ Never sign up with someone else's details; ❌ never sell/deliver a
  "ready-made account" — that's asset-fraud and instantly keylogger-brand Collapse to you
- ❌ Never promise income ("₦50k/day") — promise **research, setup, and
  education**; income depends on time invested
- ✅ Publish a tiny "realistic expectations" note: surveys = pocket money to
  "₦20–60k/month side-cash"; the big money in this ecosystem = the
  affiliate + freelance streams you tracked (Elementor/Hostinger/Fiverr)
- ✅ Re-run your daily tracker every morning so nothing you teach is stale
- ✅ Refund politely if a buyer's country/site changes — that's WHY the daily
  checker exists; use it as your defence
